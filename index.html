Who Am I

I am a Computer Engineer and graduated in 2024 with an Associate’s Degree in Health Science from Pasadena, California. I started my career in technical support for 2 years and then worked in healthcare for almost 7 years. After that, I moved into cybersecurity, where I worked as a Cybersecurity Teaching Assistant, Test Engineer, and later became a Cybersecurity Instructor. I teach military veterans and immigrants, and I also teach part-time for a college in Australia and a university in Pennsylvania, USA. I also created a course about Android Systems Security for an internship program. I later became an IT Security Manager, where I lead a small team and handle IT and security operations. I manage cloud systems in Azure and AWS, Microsoft 365, user accounts, Microsoft Defender, Google Workspace, ConnectWise tools, POS systems, and system migrations. I also worked at a Managed Service Provider as a Cybersecurity L2 and L3, where I handled incident response, patching, vulnerability assessments, and penetration testing. I have experience using tools like Splunk, SentinelOne XDR, and Blumira. I also have industry certifications, join CTF challenges on TryHackMe and Hack The Box, and contribute to Telus AI. Services

Cybersecurity, AI SEO & Automation Services

Security Operations Defend

Our Security Operations Defend service provides continuous cybersecurity monitoring, rapid threat detection, and incident response to protect businesses from cyberattacks, ransomware, malware, and unauthorized access. Using advanced security tools and experienced analysts, we identify, investigate, and respond to security incidents before they impact business operations, networks, and sensitive data.

AI SEO

AI SEO services leverage artificial intelligence to optimize website content, improve Google search rankings, and increase targeted organic traffic. By analyzing keywords, search trends, competitor strategies, and user behavior, we develop smart SEO strategies that improve online visibility, website engagement, and digital growth for businesses and brands.

Automation

Our business automation solutions streamline repetitive tasks, workflows, and operational processes to improve productivity and reduce costs. Using tools such as Zapier, n8n, and AI-powered integrations, we create intelligent automation systems that save time, reduce manual errors, and help businesses operate more efficiently.

Vulnerability Assessments

Vulnerability Assessments identify cybersecurity weaknesses in systems, applications, cloud environments, and networks before attackers can exploit them. We provide detailed security analysis, risk identification, and actionable remediation recommendations to strengthen your organization's security posture and compliance readiness.

Cybersecurity Consulting

Our Cybersecurity Consulting services provide strategic security guidance to help organizations build secure and resilient digital infrastructures. We assess cybersecurity risks, develop security frameworks, improve compliance readiness, and deliver tailored cybersecurity solutions aligned with business goals and industry best practices.

Cybersecurity Awareness Training

Cybersecurity Awareness Training helps employees recognize and prevent phishing attacks, malware infections, social engineering threats, and other cyber risks. Through engaging and practical training programs, we help organizations build a security-aware culture that minimizes human error and improves overall cybersecurity protection.
